SteadyDigital browser-prepare — hardware test

Reuses the shipping P1HidRecovery engine with the SteadyDigital v2 layout and assets. It takes a stock SteadyDigital into fpvOS USB clean recovery over WebHID. It writes only bootloader RAM; the bank-B install is a separate, already-proven step. Keep external power connected.

Step 1 — Restart stock into the bootloader

Click, then pick the SteadyDigital's Sirius serial port (1d6b:0210). It reboots into the U-Boot download gadget.

Step 2 — Load recovery into RAM

After USB reconnects, click and pick USB download gadget (1d6b:0100). Already in that mode? Start here.
